# Q3 2026 Financial & Operational Executive Summary

## 1. Executive Performance Overview
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: Closed Q3 at **$42.5 Million**, representing a **34% Year-over-Year (YoY)** growth compared to $31.7 Million in Q3 2025.
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Reached **118%**, driven primarily by expansion in enterprise AI assistant tier subscriptions.
- **Gross Margin**: Expanded to **78.5%**, up from 72.1% in Q2, reflecting successful infrastructure cost optimization.
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents**: Maintained at **$18.2 Million** with zero long-term debt and positive operating cash flow for the second consecutive quarter.

## 2. Infrastructure & LLM Unit Economics
- **Cloud Computing Expenditure**: Decreased by **14% quarter-over-quarter** despite an 80% surge in user query volume.
- **Cost Reduction Drivers**:
  - Implemented 4-bit AWQ model quantization and local vLLM serving clusters for tier-1 RAG queries, reducing external API token spend by $120,000 monthly.
  - Hybrid search caching: 32% of frequent enterprise queries served via vector cache, lowering average retrieval latency from 450ms to 65ms.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Dropped to **$14,200**, with a payback period of **8.2 months**.
